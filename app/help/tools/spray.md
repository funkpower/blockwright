Scatters stars, haze or smoke across the cells it passes.

Stars are small glyphs in the pen's foreground, placed only on open ground away from shapes; the right button takes them off. Haze and smoke lift the ramp by ░▒, lighter with the left button and darker with the right. The pattern is fixed to the canvas by its seed, so strokes over one another paint the same field. It can't paint a Pixels layer.

- **Spray**: stars, haze (long, low bands) or smoke (tall, curling wisps).
- **Density**: how many stars, or how much of the canvas haze or smoke covers.
- **Seed**: which pattern; the same seed paints the same one.
- **Ramp**: the colours haze and smoke walk; stars ignore it.
- **Size**, **Tip**, **Pressure**, **Alpha lock**, **Symmetry**: as for every brush.
