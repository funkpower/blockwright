Paints one glyph in the pen's colours wherever it passes.

The left button paints the glyph, the right erases to a blank cell. F1 to F10, or a click on the glyph bar, pick the glyph from the current F-key set; it starts as the full block █. On a Pixels layer it can paint only █, ▀, ▄ and blanks.

- **Glyph**: the glyph it paints.
- **Size**, **Tip**, **Pressure**, **Alpha lock**, **Symmetry**: as for every brush.
