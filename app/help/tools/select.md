Selects a rectangle of cells to copy, move, flip or rotate. While there is a selection, the brushes, the shapes, Fill and Gradient paint only inside it; typing with Keyboard or Text does not keep to it.

Drag to select. Drag inside the selection, or press an arrow key, to lift the cells and move them; Enter or a click outside puts them down as one edit, and Esc puts them back. Delete clears the selection. The Selection menu has Select All, Deselect, the flips and rotations, Save to Library, and Save as Frame Style, which turns a small drawn box into a style for the Frame tool.

A Library frame or band (a stamp saved with Resize set) floats with handles on its outline: drag one to resize the stamp, the corners and ends kept whole and the edges and middle repeated. A band resizes only in width. Save to Library's Resize and Insets mark a selection as a frame or band: the insets are the cells kept whole at each side.

- **Paste**: how a paste or a moved selection lands. Normal writes every cell, Transparent skips blank cells, Under writes only into blank cells.
