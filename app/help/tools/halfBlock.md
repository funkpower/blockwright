A pencil of half-block pixels: each cell is two square pixels, top and bottom, drawn with ▀ and ▄.

The left button draws in the pen's foreground, the right in its background. A drag is joined into an unbroken line.

- **Pencil 4-connected**: on, a line steps only sideways and up or down; off, it may step diagonally too.
- **Alpha lock**: paint only where the layer already has something.
- **Symmetry**: repeat the stroke mirrored or turned (Edit > Mirror Mode). Mirror X reflects it left to right about the canvas centre, Mirror Y top to bottom, and Mirror X and Y both ways. Radial turns it **Folds** times, 2 to 8, about the canvas centre, or about the cell set in **Radial centre at**.
