Picks up the ramp colours under the tip and drags them along the stroke, like a finger through paint.

Either button. Each cell of the tip carries its own value, so a wide tip drags a patch. Results are snapped back to the ramp's rungs. It can't paint a Pixels layer.

- **Strength**: how far it drags. At 100% a value is carried unchanged; lower, it fades into the cells it crosses.
- **Ramp**, **Size**, **Tip**, **Pressure**, **Alpha lock**, **Symmetry**: as for every brush.
