Stamps a texture of small lit forms along the ramp: scales, foliage, a coin hoard, brick or mottled stone.

The texture is fixed to the canvas by its seed, so overlapping strokes paint one texture with no seams. It paints over whatever the cells held. Either button. It can't paint a Pixels layer.

- **Texture**: the kind of form: Scales, Foliage, Hoard (coins), Brick or Noise (mottled stone).
- **Density**: how closely the forms pack (under 1 leaves gaps); for brick, how small the bricks are; for Noise, how fine the grain.
- **Seed**: which texture; the same seed paints the same one.
- **Light**: the direction the light comes from.
- **Ramp**, **Size**, **Tip**, **Pressure**, **Alpha lock**, **Symmetry**: as for every brush.
