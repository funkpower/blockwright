Cleans up specks: a lone cell lighter or darker than every neighbour on the ramp takes the rung most of its neighbours share.

Either button. Cells off the ramp, black, the ramp's top rung (a glint) and the cells of a Bayer gradient's dither are left alone, and a fix that would add a new lint finding is skipped.

- **Ramp**: the ramp the specks are judged on.
- **Size**, **Tip**, **Pressure**, **Alpha lock**, **Symmetry**: as for every brush.
