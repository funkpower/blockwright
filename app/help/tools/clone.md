Copies whole cells from one place to another: glyphs, colours and transparency.

Ctrl+click (Cmd+click on macOS) sets the source. A stroke then copies from the source to the cells under the brush, keeping the distance between the source and where the stroke started. Without a source it paints nothing.

- **Aligned**: keep that distance for later strokes. Off, each stroke copies from the source again.
- **Size**, **Tip**, **Pressure**, **Alpha lock**, **Symmetry**: as for every brush.
