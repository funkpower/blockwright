Builds a cell up through space, ░, ▒, ▓ and █ in the pen's colours, a step each time the brush passes.

The left button builds up, the right takes a step back down towards a space. Glyphs other than the shades, such as text and half blocks, are left alone. It can't paint a Pixels layer, which holds no shades.

- **Size**, **Tip**: how wide the brush is and whether it is square or round.
- **Pressure**: what a stylus's pressure scales.
- **Alpha lock**, **Symmetry**: as for every brush.
