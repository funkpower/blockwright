Lightens or darkens the cells under it along a colour ramp, through the ░▒▓ shades between each pair of colours.

The left button lightens, the right darkens. Cells whose colours aren't on the ramp are left alone, and it never writes a shade of two colours that aren't neighbours on the ramp. It can't paint a Pixels layer.

- **Strength**: how far a pass moves a cell. 25% is one rung, 100% a whole colour (four rungs).
- **Ramp**: the colours it steps through, dark to light.
- **Size**, **Tip**, **Pressure**, **Alpha lock**, **Symmetry**: as for every brush.
