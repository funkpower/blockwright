Shades a shape as a lit dome, cylinder or flat plane along the ramp, with a lit side, a short fall of shades and a shadow side.

The shape is the selection, or with nothing selected the cells the stroke covers. Either button. It can't paint a Pixels layer.

- **Form**: dome, cylinder or plane.
- **Light**: the direction the light comes from.
- **Form rim light**: a rung of reflected light on the edge away from the light.
- **Glint**: the ramp's top colour on a small spot at the brightest point.
- **Strength**: how far each cell moves towards its tone.
- **Ramp**, **Size**, **Tip**, **Pressure**, **Alpha lock**, **Symmetry**: as for every brush.
