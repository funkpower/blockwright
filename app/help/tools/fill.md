Floods the area of one colour under the click with the pen's colour, a half-block pixel at a time.

The left button fills with the pen's foreground, the right with its background. The area is the pixels of the clicked colour joined side by side or up and down.

- **Sample merged**: find the area by what the canvas shows, not by the active layer alone. The fill still goes into the active layer.
- **Alpha lock**, **Symmetry**: as for every drawing tool.
