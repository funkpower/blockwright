The ramp shade brush with a soft edge: it moves the middle of the tip the most and the edge a rung, in bands a cell wide.

The left button lightens, the right darkens. It can't paint a Pixels layer.

- **Hardness**: how sharp the edge is. 0% spreads the falloff across the whole tip; 100% moves every cell as much as the middle.
- **Strength**: how far the middle moves, 25% a rung and 100% a whole colour.
- **Ramp**, **Size**, **Tip**, **Pressure**, **Alpha lock**, **Symmetry**: as for every brush.
