Draws a border to fit the rectangle you drag, from the corner you press to the corner you let go at.

Each frame is a small piece cut into corners, edges and a middle: the corners stay as they are and the edges repeat along their length, so a frame draws to any size from its smallest up. A drag smaller than that grows to it. The middle of most frames is open and leaves what is under it alone. Line, half-block and shaded frames also leave the background under their own cells, so they can be dropped onto a coloured scene; the bevel, hazard stripes and button paint their own. The right button erases the cells the frame would cover.

- **Frame**: the style: single, double and mixed lines, heavy or half blocks, shaded, bevel, hazard stripes, dashed, or a button.
- **Colours**: the frame in its own colours, or recoloured to the pen's foreground and background (the foreground only, on the cells that keep the background under them).
- **Alpha lock**, **Symmetry**: as for every drawing tool.
