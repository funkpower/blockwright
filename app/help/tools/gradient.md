Fills with a gradient along the ramp, in bands of whole cells shaded ░▒▓, from where you press to where you let go.

It fills the selection, or the whole layer with nothing selected, and fits the gradient to the cells round it. The left button runs from the From rung to the To rung, the right the other way round. It can't paint a Pixels layer.

- **Gradient**: linear along the drag, or radial in rings round the press.
- **From rung**, **To rung**: where the gradient starts and ends on the ramp, 0 its darkest; four rungs make a colour.
- **Banding**: how the rungs blend. Cell-banded lays each rung in whole cells; 4×4 Bayer mixes the edge of each band into the next in an ordered dither. The lint doesn't call the dither's cells specks while each sits a rung from its neighbours, and a project keeps them that way; saved as .ans and opened again they are plain cells, and the lint calls them specks. Draw over one in another look and it is an ordinary cell again.
- **Ramp**, **Alpha lock**: as for every brush.
