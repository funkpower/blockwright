A Reference layer holds a picture to trace, saved with the project with its place, size and opacity.

It shows in its place in the stack: over the art layers below it and under the art layers above it, so raise it to see the picture over your art or lower it to draw over the picture. Painting tools cannot edit it. Alt+drag or the Move/Transform tool moves it by canvas pixels, and the Move/Transform tool's corner handles and Tool Options resize it. It is skipped by the compositor and never exported.
