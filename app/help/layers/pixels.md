A Pixels layer holds two half-block colours per cell (top and bottom), each either a palette colour or transparent.

Paint with Half-Block Brush, Line, Rectangle, Ellipse or Fill. Other tools can only write space, █, ▀ or ▄; shade-producing tools and other glyphs are refused. A Library stamp or paste of ▌ ▐ floats, but goes down only once Selection > Rotate turns them into ▀ ▄.

Set halves cover what is below; transparent halves show through. Text layers stay in front of pixels. Exports flatten these pixels into half-block glyphs and colours in the visible composite.
