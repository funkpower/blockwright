Third-party software in the macOS app

Beyond what NOTICE lists, Blockwright.app carries the following. The licence
texts are in this directory, Contents/Resources/doc in the app.

- Qt @QT_VERSION@, The Qt Company Ltd and other contributors,
  https://www.qt.io/, used under the GNU Lesser General Public License v3
  (LGPL-3.0-only.txt, with the GNU GPL v3 in LICENSE):
    frameworks in Contents/Frameworks: @QT_FRAMEWORKS@
    plugins in Contents/PlugIns: @QT_PLUGINS@
  The Qt frameworks and plugins are Qt's own release builds of unmodified Qt
  source, loaded at run time. Packaging keeps only the app's architecture in
  them, strips their local symbols (macdeployqt), points their load paths into
  the app and signs them again. They can be replaced with other builds of the
  same Qt version: copy them over the ones in Contents/Frameworks or
  Contents/PlugIns, then sign the app again, as changing a file in it breaks
  its signature:
    codesign --force --deep --sign - Blockwright.app
  Their source:
  https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/@QT_MINOR@/@QT_VERSION@/submodules/
  Qt includes third-party code under its own licences; the qt-sbom directory
  lists each component, its version and its licence.

- libzip @LIBZIP_VERSION@, Dieter Baron and Thomas Klausner,
  https://libzip.org/, BSD 3-clause licence (libzip.txt); Zstandard
  @ZSTD_VERSION@, Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates,
  https://facebook.github.io/zstd/, BSD licence (zstd.txt). Both are linked
  into the programs; libzip uses the zlib that is part of macOS.
